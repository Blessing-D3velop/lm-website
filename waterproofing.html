Our Approach

Finding the source, not just the symptom

Waterproofing only works if it addresses where water is actually entering — so we inspect roofs, walls, joints and wet areas carefully before recommending a solution.

We use proven waterproofing membranes and sealants suited to South African rainfall and roof types, applied correctly the first time.

What We Cover

Waterproofing services that hold up

Roof Waterproofing

Flat and low-slope roof waterproofing to stop leaks before they reach your ceilings.

Wall Waterproofing

External wall treatments that keep rising damp and water penetration out.

Bathroom Waterproofing

Correctly waterproofed floors and walls beneath tiling in wet areas.

Leak Prevention

Moisture Protection
