Our Approach

Preparation is what makes a paint job last

Every painting project starts with proper surface preparation — cleaning, sanding, crack filling and priming — because a great finish is only as good as what's underneath it.

We work with quality paints and coatings suited to South African weather conditions, whether it's a bedroom refresh or a full building exterior.
