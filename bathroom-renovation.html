Our Approach

One team, every trade, one finished bathroom

Bathroom renovations touch waterproofing, tiling, plumbing and finishing all at once — so we manage the whole project ourselves instead of juggling separate contractors.

The result is a bathroom that's properly waterproofed underneath and beautifully finished on top, done on a realistic timeline.
